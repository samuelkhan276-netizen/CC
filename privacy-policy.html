Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how CC (“we,” “us,” or “our”) handles personal information submitted through our Facebook lead forms and related educational-information services.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the form you complete, we may collect information you choose to provide, such as your name, phone number, email address, and answers about your interests or experience with stock-market education.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information to respond to your enquiry, contact you about educational materials and market-learning updates you request, provide customer support, and manage our records.

3. Calls and Messages

If you provide a phone number and submit a form requesting contact, we may contact you by phone, SMS, or WhatsApp about your enquiry and relevant educational information. You can ask us to stop contacting you at any time.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share it with service providers who help us operate our communications or services, or where disclosure is required by law. Those providers should use the information only for the relevant service.

5. Data Security and Retention

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information. We retain it only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, unless a longer period is required by law.

6. Your Choices and Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information you have provided, subject to applicable law. You may also withdraw your consent to further communications by replying to a message or contacting us through the Facebook page or contact channel from which you received our communication.

7. Third-Party Platforms

Lead forms may be provided by Meta platforms, including Facebook and Instagram. Your use of those platforms is also subject to Meta’s own terms and privacy policies.

8. Children’s Privacy

Our educational information and lead forms are not intended for children under 18. Please do not submit personal information if you are under 18.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated date.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact us through the official Facebook page or the contact details shown in the advertisement or communication you received from us.
